Motion

Reveals use cubic-bezier(.16,.84,.24,1); theme switches use cubic-bezier(.76,0,.24,1); durations are 280 to 600 ms. One passive scroll listener drives every scroll-linked effect, using only transform, opacity and clip-path. With reduced motion, media is static and full-bleed and numbers are final.

The orange stroke over “Strat” is the signature. It appears in four places only: above the topic label, under the active nav item, on the top edge of the highlighted panel, and as a divider between two statements.

What is AeroStrat?

AeroStrat is a next-generation aviation technology company based in Tallinn, Estonia. It is building one connected software ecosystem for airline operations: training, safety and compliance, flight operations, maintenance and engineering, and network planning. Its training products, AeroStrat EBT/CBTA and AeroStrat TMS, are live, developed and tested with two regional airlines.

Which AeroStrat products are live today?

Two training products. AeroStrat EBT/CBTA grades every pilot check against the ICAO nine-competency framework on the airline's own forms. AeroStrat TMS keeps every pilot current with validity control, renewal planning, recurrent training, records and audit. Airlines can take one, or both on a single pilot record.

What comes after training?

Safety & Compliance, Flight Operations, Maintenance & Engineering and Network Planning, each built on the same foundation as training: one crew list, one record and one login across every module.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

The short answers airlines ask for first.

What is AeroStrat?

AeroStrat is a next-generation aviation technology company based in Tallinn, Estonia. It is building one connected software ecosystem for airline operations: training, safety and compliance, flight operations, maintenance and engineering, and network planning. Its training products, AeroStrat EBT/CBTA and AeroStrat TMS, are live, developed and tested with two regional airlines.

Which AeroStrat products are live today?

Two training products. AeroStrat EBT/CBTA grades every pilot check against the ICAO nine-competency framework on the airline's own forms. AeroStrat TMS keeps every pilot current with validity control, renewal planning, recurrent training, records and audit. Airlines can take one, or both on a single pilot record.

What comes after training?

Safety & Compliance, Flight Operations, Maintenance & Engineering and Network Planning, each built on the same foundation as training: one crew list, one record and one login across every module.

Which regulators does AeroStrat support?

AeroStrat is built on ICAO, IATA and EASA guidance and is not tied to one regulator. Each airline is set up to its own manuals and national authority, including EASA and ECAA frameworks.

Who uses AeroStrat today?

AeroStrat has been tested for more than 8 months in co-development with two regional airlines: 120+ pilots and 250+ cabin crew, with more than 14,000 grades recorded to date.

About us

Aviation experience, built into technology.

AeroStrat is an aviation technology company based in Tallinn, Estonia. Our team pairs 25+ years of experience in the aviation sector with deep technology expertise. We are developing technology across the airline, and we started with training: AeroStrat EBT/CBTA and AeroStrat TMS are live today.

Vision

To establish the definitive digital operating system for global airlines, unifying fragmented data into a single source of operational intelligence and continuous oversight.

Mission

To engineer intuitive, enterprise-grade technology that eliminates operational silos, transforming complex data into connected intelligence so airlines can optimise performance, elevate safety and drive continuous improvement across their entire ecosystem.

Our direction

One domain at a time, on one shared foundation.

An airline runs on connected disciplines. AeroStrat is building technology for each of them. Training is live; the next domains follow on the same foundation.

01Live Training AeroStrat EBT/CBTA for competency assessment and AeroStrat TMS for training management, on one pilot record.

How we work with you

Set up from your manuals.

AeroStrat staff onboard each airline from its own manuals. We build your check forms from your paper forms, set your grading scale and document control, and configure the platform to your authority. Building your forms is part of the implementation service, not a self-serve builder.

Your forms stay as they are, whether they start on paper or in digital form. Support and maintenance included.

Privacy notice.

How AeroStrat uses the personal data you send us through this website. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Who we are

The controller of your personal data is AeroStrat OÜ, registry code 17638511, Tornimäe tn 5, 10145 Tallinn, Estonia. For any privacy question, write to info@aero-strat.com.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive your name and email address, and, if you add them, your airline, your role, the product and EBT stage you selected and your message. When you email us, we receive what you write and your email address.

The website itself sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising trackers and does not build visitor profiles. Like any website, our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and, if you ask for one, arrange a walkthrough. The legal basis is the steps you ask us to take before a possible contract and our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • To keep the website available and secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a safe website (Article 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell your data, use it for automated decisions about you or add you to a mailing list.

Who receives it

Only service providers that help us run the website and our email, each under a data processing agreement:

  • Vercel hosts the website and runs the contact form.
  • Resend delivers the contact form to our mailbox.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email.
  • unpkg serves open-source code files the pages load in your browser.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer relies on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and the related correspondence for as long as needed to follow up on them and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless the enquiry leads to a contract, in which case contract records are kept as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your personal data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Write to info@aero-strat.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee) or to the authority in the country where you live or work.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this notice and the date at the top.

# EBT and CBTA glossary

> Plain-English definitions of the terms airline training departments use for evidence-based training (EBT) and competency-based training and assessment (CBTA), from the nine pilot competencies to Baseline EBT and training records.

Canonical page: https://aero-strat.com/glossary

## Training approaches

### Evidence-Based Training (EBT)

A way of running recurrent training and assessment for airline pilots, set out by ICAO in its Manual of Evidence-based Training (Doc 9995). Instead of repeating the same list of manoeuvres every check, EBT develops and assesses a pilot's overall capability across the [core competencies](https://aero-strat.com/glossary#competencies), and uses operational and safety data to decide what is trained. [AeroStrat EBT/CBTA](https://aero-strat.com/ebt-cbta) grades every check this way.

### Competency-Based Training and Assessment (CBTA)

Training and assessment built around defined competencies and the standard of performance expected for each, rather than hours completed or items ticked off. ICAO describes the approach in PANS-TRG (Doc 9868). EBT applies CBTA to recurrent pilot training; CBTA also covers cabin crew, instructors and other aviation roles.

### The nine pilot competencies

The competencies ICAO and IATA use to describe pilot performance. Each one is graded on its own, so a training department can see exactly where a pilot, a cohort or a fleet needs work. Application of knowledge (KNO) was added to the original eight.

1. **PRO:** Application of procedures and compliance with regulations
2. **KNO:** Application of knowledge
3. **COM:** Communication
4. **FPA:** Flight path management, automation
5. **FPM:** Flight path management, manual control
6. **LTW:** Leadership and teamwork
7. **PSD:** Problem solving and decision making
8. **SAW:** Situation awareness and management of information
9. **WLM:** Workload management

### Competency

A dimension of human performance that reliably predicts successful performance on the job. A competency shows in behaviours that draw on the relevant knowledge, skills and attitudes, and it is observed and assessed under set conditions.

### Competency framework

The set of competencies, descriptions and observable behaviours an airline uses for training and assessment. ICAO publishes a reference framework; each airline adapts it to its own operation and has it accepted by its authority. AeroStrat EBT/CBTA is set up to the airline's own adapted framework and manual.

### Observable behaviour (OB)

A single, role-related behaviour an instructor can see during a session and record as evidence for a competency grade. Each competency is described by several observable behaviours, for example confirming that information has been understood, under Communication.

### Threat and Error Management (TEM)

A model for describing how crews manage operational risk. Threats are events outside the crew's control that make the flight more complex, such as weather or a technical fault. Errors are crew actions or omissions that lead away from what was intended. An undesired aircraft state is a position, speed or configuration that reduces the safety margin. EBT scenarios are built and debriefed with TEM.

## The EBT programme

### EBT module

One recurrent training and assessment event in the simulator, normally made up of an [evaluation phase](https://aero-strat.com/glossary#eval), [manoeuvres training](https://aero-strat.com/glossary#mt) and [scenario-based training](https://aero-strat.com/glossary#sbt). Modules follow a multi-year programme, usually three years, so that every competency and training topic is covered.

### Evaluation phase (EVAL)

The first phase of an EBT module: a realistic line scenario flown with as little instructor input as possible. It assesses the crew's competencies and shows what the rest of the module should focus on.

### Manoeuvres training (MT)

The phase of an EBT module where specific handling manoeuvres, such as an engine failure on take-off or a go-around, are practised until they are flown to the required standard.

### Scenario-based training (SBT)

The phase of an EBT module that develops competencies through realistic line scenarios, focused on the training needs found in the evaluation phase. Scenarios are built around threats and errors (see [TEM](https://aero-strat.com/glossary#tem)).

### Day 1 and Day 2

In a programme run on EBT principles, the Day 1 proficiency check comes first and the Day 2 training session is planned from what Day 1 found: "check, then train".

### Flight simulation training device (FSTD)

An approved simulator, such as a full flight simulator, used for pilot training and checking. EBT modules are flown in a qualified FSTD.

## Implementation stages

### Conventional training and checking (Stage 0)

Traditional recurrent training: "train, then check". Each item is graded satisfactory or unsatisfactory, or pass or fail, against flight tolerances on the authority's form. Most airlines still start here. [AeroStrat EBT/CBTA](https://aero-strat.com/ebt-cbta) supports it natively.

### EBT principles (Stage 1)

Conventional checking run with EBT methods: "check, then train". A [Day 1](https://aero-strat.com/glossary#day1-day2) check comes first, Day 2 training is planned from its findings, and the nine competencies are graded 1 to 5 as the airline's manual defines.

### Mixed EBT (Stage 2)

EBT running alongside the conventional programme. Modules include an evaluation phase, manoeuvres and scenario-based training, but the licence is still revalidated by a proficiency check.

### Baseline EBT (Stage 3)

Recurrent training and assessment fully dedicated to EBT. Licence revalidation runs through the EBT programme instead of a separate proficiency check.

### Enhanced EBT

Baseline EBT adapted with the airline's own data, such as flight data and safety reports, so training targets the risks of that specific operation.

## Grading, records and training management

### Grading scale

The scale an instructor uses to grade each competency. EBT commonly uses five points, 1 to 5, as defined in the airline's manual. AeroStrat EBT/CBTA uses the airline's own scale and scoring rules; its default bands run from S1 Unsatisfactory to S5 Excellent. The instructor always sets the final grade.

### Instructor concordance

How closely instructors agree when they grade the same performance, also called inter-rater reliability. Training departments monitor it to keep grading fair and consistent. AeroStrat EBT/CBTA shows each instructor's average grade, updated with every submission.

### Remedial training

Extra training given when a competency is not demonstrated to the required standard, before the pilot returns to the normal programme.

### Training management system (TMS)

Software that plans and tracks crew training: who needs which check or course and when, each person's licences, medicals and validity, and the records an authority audits. [AeroStrat TMS](https://aero-strat.com/tms) does this, configured to the airline's OM-D.

### Validity

The period during which a licence, rating, medical, check or course stays valid. Once it expires, the crew member cannot operate until it is renewed. [AeroStrat TMS](https://aero-strat.com/tms) lists renewals by status: green, yellow, red, expired or missing.

### Electronic training record (ETR)

A digital record of a crew member's training and assessments that replaces paper files. It has to meet the authority's rules on retention and integrity, which is why every change is logged.

### Operator and licence proficiency checks (OPC / LPC)

The simulator checks of conventional recurrent training: the operator proficiency check (OPC) and the licence proficiency check (LPC). In Baseline EBT the EBT programme takes their place for licence revalidation.

### Line check

A check flown on a normal revenue flight to confirm that a pilot operates to the airline's standards on the line.

### Operations Manual Part D (OM-D)

The part of an airline's Operations Manual that sets out its training programmes, checks and standards. [AeroStrat TMS](https://aero-strat.com/tms) is configured to it.

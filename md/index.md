# AeroStrat: Intelligence Driven Aviation

> AeroStrat (Intelligence Driven Aviation) is building the next generation of aviation technology: one connected ecosystem for airline operations, spanning training, safety and compliance, flight operations, maintenance and engineering, and network planning, on a single shared foundation set up to each airline's own manuals and authority. Its team pairs 25 years of experience in the aviation sector with deep technology expertise. AeroStrat started with pilot training, where two products are live with airlines today: AeroStrat EBT/CBTA for evidence-based competency assessment and grading, and AeroStrat TMS for training planning, validity tracking and records. Based in Tallinn, Estonia.

## Vision and ecosystem

- **Vision:** To establish the definitive digital operating system for global airlines, unifying fragmented data into a single source of operational intelligence and continuous oversight.
- **Mission:** To engineer intuitive, enterprise-grade technology that eliminates operational silos, transforming complex data into connected intelligence so airlines can optimize performance, elevate safety and drive continuous improvement across their entire ecosystem.

AeroStrat is building one connected technology ecosystem for airline operations on a single shared foundation: one crew list, one record and one login across every module. Training is live; the next domains follow on the same foundation.

1. **Training (live):** AeroStrat EBT/CBTA for competency assessment and AeroStrat TMS for training management, on one pilot record.
2. **Safety & Compliance (next):** occurrence reporting and audit findings tied back to the competency evidence behind them.
3. **Flight Operations (next):** crew qualification, currency and rostering constraints in the same record as training.
4. **Maintenance & Engineering (next):** technical training and authorisation tracking on the shared competency framework.
5. **Network Planning (next):** capacity and crew readiness modelled against the training pipeline.

## The training solution

Two connected products for airline pilot training: one measures how well pilots perform, the other keeps every pilot current. Airlines can take one, or both on a single pilot record.

### AeroStrat EBT/CBTA: every grade consistent, every skill measured

Evidence-based training and assessment, graded by the ICAO, IATA and EASA method on forms built from the airline's own manual.

- Nine ICAO/IATA competencies and all 73 observable behaviours
- Day 2 training planned from the pilot's Day 1 evidence
- Trends by pilot, cohort, fleet and instructor

Details: https://aero-strat.com/ebt-cbta

### AeroStrat TMS: every pilot current, every renewal planned

The training management system: validity, planning, recurrent training, records and audit in one place.

- Every licence, medical, check and course tracked live
- Renewals planned months ahead, pilots reminded before anything lapses
- One digital file per pilot, ready for the inspector

Details: https://aero-strat.com/tms

### One pilot record: entered once, never re-typed

For airlines that take both, the products share one crew list, one pilot record and one login.

- A graded check updates the record and sets the next expiry
- Compliance and competence side by side for the Director
- One audit trail across both products

Built on ICAO, IATA and EASA guidance, set up to each airline's manuals and authority.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is AeroStrat?**
AeroStrat is a next-generation aviation technology company based in Tallinn, Estonia. It is building one connected software ecosystem for airline operations: training, safety and compliance, flight operations, maintenance and engineering, and network planning. Its training products, AeroStrat EBT/CBTA and AeroStrat TMS, are live with airlines today.

**Which AeroStrat products are live today?**
Two training products. AeroStrat EBT/CBTA grades every pilot check against the ICAO nine-competency framework on the airline's own forms. AeroStrat TMS keeps every pilot current with validity control, renewal planning, recurrent training, records and audit. Airlines can take one, or both on a single pilot record.

**What comes after training?**
Safety & Compliance, Flight Operations, Maintenance & Engineering and Network Planning, each built on the same foundation as training: one crew list, one record and one login across every module.

**Which regulators does AeroStrat support?**
AeroStrat is built on ICAO, IATA and EASA guidance and is not tied to one regulator. Each airline is set up to its own manuals and national authority, including EASA and ECAA frameworks.

**Who uses AeroStrat today?**
Founding reference client AlMasria Universal Airlines, with 120+ pilots and 250+ cabin crew and more than 14,000 grades recorded to date.

## Developed with airlines

AeroStrat EBT/CBTA and AeroStrat TMS were developed and tested with two regional airlines for more than eight months: 120+ pilots, 250+ cabin crew and 14,000+ grades recorded.

## How a pilot project works

1. **One fleet:** start with one fleet and the products you need.
2. **Success measures agreed in advance:** you and AeroStrat agree what the pilot project has to show before it starts.
3. **Share, then go live:** share your manuals, records and forms; AeroStrat sets up the rest, in about five weeks.

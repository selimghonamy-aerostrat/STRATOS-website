# About AeroStrat

> AeroStrat is a next-generation aviation technology company based in Tallinn, Estonia, building one connected ecosystem for airline operations: training, safety, fleet management and fleet operations. Its team pairs 25 years of experience in the aviation sector with deep technology expertise. It started with training, where AeroStrat EBT/CBTA and AeroStrat TMS are live today.

Canonical page: https://aero-strat.com/about

- **Vision:** To establish the definitive digital operating system for global airlines, unifying fragmented data into a single source of operational intelligence and continuous oversight.
- **Mission:** To engineer intuitive, enterprise-grade technology that eliminates operational silos, transforming complex data into connected intelligence so airlines can optimize performance, elevate safety and drive continuous improvement across their entire ecosystem.

## Who we are

- **Experience:** 25 years of experience in the aviation sector. Flight operations, crew training, regulation and oversight, seen from inside the airline.
- **Expertise:** Deep technology. Software engineering, data and analytics, delivered as secure systems an airline can rely on every day.
- **Base:** Tallinn, Estonia. An aviation technology company working with airlines and their authorities.
- **In production:** AlMasria Universal Airlines. Founding reference client: 120+ pilots and 250+ cabin crew, with more than 14,000 grades recorded to date.

## Products

- **AeroStrat EBT/CBTA** (live): competency assessment and training records. It captures, grades and analyses evaluation data against the ICAO/IATA competency framework.
- **AeroStrat TMS** (live): validity, planning, recurrent training, records and audit in one system, sharing one crew list and one pilot record with AeroStrat EBT/CBTA.
- **What they are not:** neither product delivers e-learning courses. They record, plan and analyse training and assessment.

## Direction: one domain at a time, on one shared foundation

An airline runs on connected disciplines. AeroStrat is building technology for each of them. Training is live; the next domains follow on the same foundation.

1. **Training (live):** AeroStrat EBT/CBTA for competency assessment and AeroStrat TMS for training management, on one pilot record.
2. **Safety & Compliance (next):** occurrence reporting and audit findings tied back to the competency evidence behind them.
3. **Flight Operations (next):** crew qualification, currency and rostering constraints in the same record as training.
4. **Maintenance & Engineering (next):** technical training and authorisation tracking on the shared competency framework.
5. **Network Planning (next):** capacity and crew readiness modelled against the training pipeline.

## How we work with you: set up from your manuals

AeroStrat staff onboard each airline from its own manuals. AeroStrat builds the airline's check forms from its paper forms, sets its grading scale and document control, and configures the platform to its authority. Building forms is part of the implementation service, not a self-serve builder. Forms stay as they are, whether they start on paper or in digital form. Support and maintenance are included.

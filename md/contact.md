# Contact AeroStrat

Canonical page: https://aero-strat.com/contact

Book a walkthrough, send one of your check forms and see how it grades in AeroStrat, or see AeroStrat TMS on a sample of your own crew and training data. A walkthrough takes 30 minutes, and we reply within one business day.

- **Book a time:** https://aero-strat.com/contact#book (30-minute walkthrough on Google Meet, Sunday to Thursday, 09:00–17:00 Cairo time, booked through Google Calendar)
- **Email:** info@aero-strat.com
- **Location:** Tallinn, Estonia
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/aero-strat/
- **Contact form:** https://aero-strat.com/contact (full name, airline, role, email, product of interest, current EBT stage, message)

## What a first call covers

- **For AeroStrat TMS:** your crew list, your current training records and the training rules in your OM-D.
- **For AeroStrat EBT/CBTA:** your EBT stage and plans, your check forms, and what AeroStrat needs from you to set up: your forms and competency mapping, your standards, your instructor ranks, and the training managers who receive alerts.

## What a first call covers

- **For STRATOS TMS:** your crew list, your current training records and the training rules in your OM-D.
- **For STRATOS EBT/CBTA:** your EBT stage and plans, your check forms, and what STRATOS needs from you to set up: your forms and competency mapping, your standards, your instructor ranks, and the training managers who receive alerts.

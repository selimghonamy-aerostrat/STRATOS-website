## Post 2: Why the grade is not the hard part

Most airline training tools record the grade. The analysis still happens by hand, in spreadsheets, weeks later.

AeroStrat EBT/CBTA grades every check against the nine ICAO/IATA pilot competencies on the airline's own forms. Pilot, cohort and fleet trends are ready the moment the instructor submits.

The instructor always sets the final grade. Any override needs a written reason, and AeroStrat never overwrites it.

See how it works: https://aero-strat.com/ebt-cbta

## Post 3: Where is your airline on the EBT path?

Conventional checking → EBT principles → Mixed EBT → Baseline EBT.

Most airlines are somewhere in between, and the terms get confusing fast: observable behaviours, EVAL, MT, SBT, Day 1 / Day 2, OPC/LPC…

We wrote a plain-English glossary of the terms training departments use every day, including the nine pilot competencies and each implementation stage:
https://aero-strat.com/glossary

Save it for your next training review meeting. 📌

## Post 4: Validity without spreadsheets

Licences, medicals, checks, courses: every one has an expiry date, and a missed one grounds a crew member.

AeroStrat TMS lists every renewal by status (green, yellow, red, expired or missing), plans recurrent training and keeps an audit-ready record. It is configured to your OM-D, not the other way round.

https://aero-strat.com/tms

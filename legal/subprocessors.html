Sub-processors

AeroStrat OÜ · registry code 17638511 · Last updated: 17 November 2026

AeroStrat uses the providers below to run AeroStrat Training. Each one may process airline data, including crew personal data, on AeroStrat's behalf and on its documented instructions. Each is bound by a data processing agreement at least as protective as AeroStrat's own commitments to its customers.

Changes to this list

AeroStrat gives customers at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor. Customers may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If an objection cannot be resolved, the customer may end the affected services under its agreement with AeroStrat.

Each provider's current certifications and audit reports are listed on its own trust and compliance page, linked in the table above.

To receive notice of changes, e-mail support@aero-strat.com with the subject "Subscribe to sub-processor updates".
